## Biography

{{ site.data.lab.pi.bio }}

His work investigates how enzyme sequence, structure, and local environment jointly control catalytic activity, stability, and selectivity. Research interests include integrated biocatalysis and chemocatalysis, rational enzyme engineering, electrochemical catalysis, computational chemistry and biology, and food biotechnology.

## Education and Appointments

- **September 2023–present** · Associate Professor, School of Food Science and Engineering, South China University of Technology.
- **August 2018–July 2023** · Ph.D., Department of Chemical Engineering, Tsinghua University. Advisor: Prof. Jun Ge.
- **September 2021–September 2022** · Joint doctoral training, Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, University of Toronto. Advisor: Prof. Edward H. Sargent.
- **August 2014–July 2018** · Bachelor’s degree, Department of Chemical Engineering, Tsinghua University.

## Research Interests

- Multiscale enzyme catalyst design and confinement effects.
- Mechanism-guided and AI-assisted protein and enzyme engineering.
- Chemoenzymatic and electrobiocatalytic synthesis.
- Sustainable biosynthesis and food biotechnology.
