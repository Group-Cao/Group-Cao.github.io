date: 2026-10-16

Our collaborative study, **Metabolic Engineering of Saccharomyces cerevisiae for Flavonoid Synthesis from Acetate**, is online in the *Journal of Agricultural and Food Chemistry*.

The work combines adaptive laboratory evolution and metabolic engineering to connect acetate utilization with flavonoid biosynthesis in yeast.

[Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jafc.6c05419).
